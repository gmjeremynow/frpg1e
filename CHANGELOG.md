# Changelog

What each version added or changed, newest first. Versions before 0.16.0 are not listed separately.

- **Names easier to read (0.48.5).** The name at the top of character, NPC and monster sheets is always dark ink on the sheet, and selected text in it shows dark with light letters instead of washing out. A name too long for its field is set smaller (down to 15px) so the whole name shows; it refits as you type and when the window is resized, and hovering shows the full name.
- **Monster art keeps your own images (0.48.4).** Apply Monster Art replaces a portrait or token image only when it is the default skull (or mystery man, or blank) or an image from the monster art folder. Anything you set by hand stays, in the book compendia, on world monsters and on placed tokens; before, the book compendia were overwritten whenever a matching "Token ..." image existed. Rebuilding the book compendia now carries hand-set monster images over to the new copies. Nothing is ever deleted.
- **Background art (0.48.3).** The system now has its own background picture (adventurers at a ruin entrance), shown on its card in Foundry's setup screen. A world can use it by setting its Background Image to `systems/frpg1e/assets/background.webp`.
- **Weapon proficiency list takes module weapons (0.48.2).** Modules can add weapons to the weapon proficiency drop-down with `registerWeaponNames` (API version 3); the OA modules add the OA weapons. The list is now in alphabetical order, and a name already listed in another letter case is not repeated.
- **"Other (set below)" removed from the class list (0.48.1).** The catch-all class is no longer offered when choosing a class. A character who already has it keeps it, with its combat table, saves and hit die settings. The system still uses its defaults behind the scenes when a character's class belongs to a module that is switched off.
- **"When found" rolls happen once (0.48.0).** An item's "Determine type (when found)" dice are rolled automatically the moment it is added to a character (dragged on, or given by the treasure roller), and the result is kept on the item: it is never rolled again, and Show/Use no longer offers it. The result appears under the item's name in Magic Items and on its sheet. A Bag of Holding gets its weight and capacity this way; items with charges get their charges. Items owned before this version whose roll already shows (charges or capacity set) are marked as found; any others show a Roll button for their single roll. Containers in Magic Items (Bag of Holding, Portable Hole, Handy Haversack and so on) now show "holds X / Y gp", turning red when over capacity; hover it to see what is inside. A container's own sheet shows what it is holding now.
- **Load note (0.47.9).** The note about carried or worn items with no weight now names them, since magic items have no weight column to show a ?.
- **Anything items (0.47.8).** Anything Armor and Sword (Anything) (UA) are now real armor and a weapon, so they can be worn or wielded: Anything Armor starts as Leather Armor +1 (the most common +1 armor on the DMG armor table) and counts half when worn; Sword (Anything) starts as a long sword +1 (DMG p.124: 70% of swords are long swords). Change construction or sword type to match what was found. Anything Item weighs 3 (a small miscellaneous item, 1-5). Owned copies are replaced once on load, keeping their location.
- **Stone Horse and Underwater Apparatus (0.47.7).** DM estimates: Stone Horse 20,000, Underwater Apparatus 8,000.
- **Cyclocone and Prison of Binding (0.47.6).** The Cyclocone looks like an ordinary pointed hat (UA), so like other hats it is Not counted. The Prison of Binding looks like a small birdcage (UA) and weighs 50, as the DSG Table 35 birdcage.
- **DM weights for misc magic items (0.47.5).** 51 misc magic items and artifacts with no weight in any 1e book, OSRIC 3 or 2e now carry weights set by the DM (e.g. gauntlets 10, Gauntlets of Ogre Power 25, braziers 50, bowls 30, censers 20, dusts 5, folded Portable Hole 5, Saw of Mighty Cutting 300, Iron Flask of Tuerny 100; Teeth of Dahlver-Nar 1 per tooth). Not counted: Mystical Spell Component Case (insignificant), Sphere of Annihilation (not a carried object), and the Mighty Servant of Leuk-O, Mystical Organ, Throne of the Gods and Walking Hut (not carried). Still Unspecified: items whose weight depends on a roll or choice (Bag of Holding, Hornblade, Pole Arm +1, Carpet of Flying, the two Rugs).
- **Magic armor and bows (0.47.4).** Worn magic armor counts half its weight toward your load (DMG p.28); the armor list shows the halved figure, and carried spare armor counts in full. Magic shields keep their full weight (DMG p.28). Bow +1 is replaced by four items, Long Bow +1, Short Bow +1, Composite Long Bow +1 and Composite Short Bow +1, each with that bow's statistics and the same weight as the non-magical bow (DMG p.225: 100, 50, 80, 50). The DMG leaves the type of bow to the DM, so the treasure roller picks one of the four at random and says so; swap it if it doesn't suit. An owned Bow +1 (which used long bow statistics) gets the long bow's 100 gp. Inn meals are Not counted.
- **More weights (0.47.3).** Where no 1e book gives a weight, OSRIC 3 and then AD&D 2nd edition were checked, converting pounds at 10 gp each; each item cites its source. OSRIC 3 Player Guide: sling 5 (p.78; also Sling of Seeking), bracers 1 (p.74, leather bracer), Pipes of the Sewers, Recorder of Ye'Cind and the chimes 10 (p.74, musical instrument). 2e PHB: hand crossbow 30 (p.94), staff sling 20 (p.95), harness 100, horseshoes of speed and of a zephyr 100, nets of entrapment and snaring 50 (p.91). 2e DMG: horse grain 100 per day (p.166). Magic crossbows 50: the DMG (p.168) says 10% are heavy, so they default to light (change to 80 for a heavy one). Gloves (OSRIC lists gloves as not adding to encumbrance), hats and caps are Not counted. 73 items, mostly misc magic items and artifacts, have no weight in any of these books and stay Unspecified.
- **Weights filled in (0.47.2).** 357 items with no published weight now carry a Derived weight from a like item in DMG Appendix O, the PHB or UA weapon tables, or the DSG equipment table (potions 25, rods 60, staves 100, wands 30, rings and small jewelry 3, scrolls 25, books 200, cloaks and robes 25, helms 45, horns 50, and so on); each item's Weight citation names the item it was compared to. Set by the DM: girdles 9 (3× a belt), incense stick 1, blowgun needle 0.1, large iron box 30, hat and cap 5, bit and bridle 15, Mirror of Life Trapping 300. 92 items with no honest comparison stay Unspecified (shown as ?). Fixed: bows and crossbows had 1-2 gp; they now use Appendix O (composite long 80, composite short 50, long 100, short 50; heavy crossbow 80, light 50). The hand crossbow, slings, staff slings, Bow +1, the three magic crossbows and the Sling of Seeking have no printed weight (or no stated type) and are now Unspecified. Weights accept decimals; totals round to 0.1 gp. Owned items are updated once on load; weights a GM entered are kept.
- **Weight sources (0.47.1).** Every item has a Weight source and citation (item sheet, beside the weight): Published (printed in a 1e book, e.g. DMG p.225), Derived (a midpoint of a printed range, or a printed rule of thumb), Set by the DM (filled in automatically when you type a weight onto an item with none), Unspecified (no published weight), or Not counted (DMG p.225: material components, thieves' picks; animals and vehicles are not carried). Unspecified weights show as a red ? in Possessions and are not counted; the load line says how many carried or worn items have no weight. No weight is guessed: only printed weights were added (barding, UA p.76; Rod of Lordly Might, Rope of Climbing, Mattock and Maul of the Titans, Machine of Lum, DMG). Dragon #35's encumbrance table (the 1980 errata) is the same as DMG Appendix O, so nothing changed from it. Container capacities for the backpack (400 gp), belt pouches (200 / 100), saddlebags (1,000 / 250), boxes (200 / 50) and wooden chests (15 gp per gp of the chest's weight) come from Sage Advice, Dragon #142 p.71, which calls them unofficial; each item's notes say so. The PHB iron boxes now use the Sage Advice box capacities and have no weight (the iron chest weight they borrowed is gone). Magic armor and weapons keep their base weights and are not yet classified.
- **Containers, animals and vehicles (0.47.0).** Gear and magic items can be marked as holding things (item sheet, Container box): an ordinary container, a magic container, or an animal or vehicle. Each one you own appears in every Location dropdown ("In Backpack", "On Mule"). An ordinary container weighs its own weight plus its contents and counts wherever it sits; a magic container weighs its own weight (the Pouch of Accessibility rises from 10 to 40 gp as it fills); anything on an animal or vehicle does not count toward your load. Loads show beside each container and in the Load column of Animals & Transport, flagged when over. Capacities come from the books only: large sack 400 gp and small sack 100 gp (DMG p.225 example); animals from WSG Table 13 (riding horse uses the wild horse, 3,000 / 6,000); Handy Haversack 1,200, Pouch of Accessibility 3,000, Girdle of Many Pouches 6,400 (UA); the Bag of Holding's weight and capacity are set by its when-found Capacity roll. Backpacks, pouches, saddlebags, chests, boxes, cases and quivers have no printed weight limit, so none is checked. Carts and wagons depend on the team (WSG Table 14, in their notes); enter the maximum on the item. Items already owned get their container settings once when the world loads.
- **Location replaces the Worn boxes (0.46.1).** Armor and magic items set to Worn are worn (armor class, protection). Livestock and transport sit in their own Animals & Transport list with no location and no weight toward the load. Gear has separate Cost and Notes; notes can be edited in the list. Iron boxes and wooden chests get DMG Appendix O weights (midpoint of the printed range, range in notes); the saddle, herbs and thieves' picks carry DMG notes.
- **Item location (0.46.0).** Every item has a Location: Worn, Carried, On mount, or Stored. Only worn and carried items count toward encumbrance; worn armor always counts as worn. The Location dropdown is in the Possessions, Weapons, Armor & Shields, Magic Items, and Gems, Jewelry & Valuables lists (weapon forms of magic items follow the magic item). For armor and worn magic items, ticking Worn sets Location to Worn, and moving one to the mount or storage unticks Worn. The Wealth box has a second row of coins stored elsewhere, which do not count. The load line shows worn, carried and coins, plus the total on the mount (to compare with the animal's load, WSG Table 13) and what is stored.
- **Spell folders (0.45.0).** The spell compendia are foldered class › level (Cleric › 1st level, Cleric › Cantrips ...). In the Items sidebar, the GM button “Spells by class and level” copies every spell from the Item compendia (the system's and any class module's) into the world as Spells › class › Level N; a spell repeated on the same list and level is copied once (the system's copy first). Running it again replaces the whole Spells folder. These are world copies, so a compendium rebuild does not change them. Also available as game.frpg1e.buildSpellFolders().
- **Magic items used as weapons (0.44.0).** Staves, some rods, the tridents, the titans' mattock and maul, the javelins of lightning and piercing, the Robe of Stars' stars, the hornblade, the Buckler Wand's dagger, the Wand of Force's energy blade, and the Mace of Cuthbert and Axe of the Dwarvish Lords (data/magic-weapons.json). When one is put on a character, a linked entry for each weapon form is added to the Weapons list (base weapon from the weapon tables plus the item's magic bonus, or the item's own damage where the book gives it; no weight of its own) and removed with the item. Random-version items (Staff of the Serpent, Staff of the Woodlands, Staff-Spear, hornblade) ask which version, or roll it. A sword button on the Magic Items list re-adds a missing entry. The DMG gives only ranges for the mattock (5-30) and maul (10-40); 5d6 and 10d4 are used. The Wand of Orcus is left out (no damage given). Charges on the Magic Items list are shown as text; change them on the item's sheet.
- **Charges when found (0.44.0).** Rods, staves and wands roll their charges with “Determine type (when found)”: rods 50 minus d10−1, staves 25 minus d6−1, wands 100 minus d20−1 (DMG p.132); tridents of fish command and submission 17-20. Left out: items with no charges or no stated dice (Rod of Absorption, Rod of Cancellation, Staff of the Serpent, Staff-Mace, Staff-Spear, Buckler Wand, Staff of Swarming Insects, Anything Wand).
- **UA items with rolled properties (0.43.1).** The UA has no artifacts using the DMG power tables, but some items roll a property. These now have table rolls in their Dice box, like the DMG's Bag of Tricks: when found, Ring of Clumsiness (secondary power, UA p.92), Boots of Varied Tracks (track prints, roll 4 times, UA p.98) and Scarab Versus Golems (golems affected, with xp and gp values, UA p.102); each use, Deck of Illusions (card drawn, UA p.99) and Spoon of Stirring (new potion from Table III.A or III.A.2, UA p.84 and 103).
- **Artifact powers and effects (0.43.0).** The DMG's six Artifacts and Relics Powers/Effects Tables (p.162-164) are in data/artifacts.json, with each artifact's slots from its entry (p.155-162). A GM-only box on an artifact's item sheet rolls all its powers once (no entry repeated on one item; this is the system's choice) and then locks them; the ethos roll is likewise made once. It records them on that copy of the item (flags.frpg1e.artifact). The Crown, Orb and Sceptre of Might roll their ethos (d20) and the Crown lists its 2nd- and 3rd-item-of-set powers; the Orbs of Dragonkind choose which of the 8 orbs; the Cup and Talisman, Rod of Seven Parts and Teeth of Dahlver-Nar list their parts; the Horn of Change rolls a power or effect each time it is blown (75% suggested chance of a power). Results can be whispered to the GM. The tables are also in the “Artifact & Relic Tables (DMG)” roll table compendium. The DMG printing used lists the Teeth's four 'Set of' rows twice; they are used once.
- **Encumbrance and movement (0.42.0).** The load is every carried item's weight × quantity plus 1 gp per coin (10 coins to the pound, PHB p.9). World setting “Encumbrance and movement”:
  - **PHB (default):** PHB p.101 levels at 350 / 700 / 1,050 gp (normal, heavy, very heavy) and up to 1,500 gp encumbered (DMG p.225), each limit raised (or lowered) by the strength weight allowance (PHB p.9; adding it to every limit is this system's reading). Movement is the base rate × 1, 3/4, 1/2, 1/4 (12" / 9" / 6" / 3" for a 12" base; the PHB's encumbered 3" to 4" is taken as 3"). Over the most, the character cannot move. The sheet shows the PHB reaction and initiative effect as a note.
  - **Wilderness Survival Guide:** Table 11 (WSG p.30) limits by strength score. Movement is scaled from Table 12's normal-terrain column against Light (moderate 10/12, heavy 7/12, severe 5/12); not encumbered is not made faster than the base rate. Over the Severe limit the character cannot move. The sheet shows Table 12's overland miles for the category.
  - **Off:** carried weight does not change movement.
  The final movement is the slower of the armor's base movement and the encumbrance movement (armor weight counts toward the load). DMG p.225's exclusions (one set of clothing, a helm worn with armor, spell components, thieves' picks) are not applied automatically: set those items' weight to 0.
- **Critical hits and fumbles (0.41.15, house rule).** The PHB and DMG have no such rule. With the world setting “Natural 20 critical hit, natural 1 fumble” (on by default), a natural 20 on an attack roll (weapon or brawl) always hits and the card is marked CRITICAL HIT; a natural 1 always misses and is marked FUMBLE. What either does beyond that is left to the DM.
- **Gem icons (0.41.13, redrawn 0.41.14).** Each DMG gem has an icon in assets/gems, drawn as a flat white silhouette like Foundry's own item icons: a cut stone for transparent gems, a rounded stone for opaque ones, with bands, flecks, rings, a star or opal spots where the DMG description calls for them, and a pearl; jewelry has a ring. They replace icons/svg/jewel.svg, which Foundry does not provide. Accept the compendium rebuild to update the Gems compendium; gems and jewelry already in the world or on characters are fixed once on load.
- **Token names follow renames (0.41.11).** Renaming an actor renames its prototype token, so tokens dropped afterwards carry the new name, and tokens of that actor already on scenes that showed the old name are renamed too. Characters always follow the actor's name; NPCs and monsters only when the token name matched the old name (a token deliberately called something else is left alone). On first load, characters whose token name no longer matches are brought in line once.
- **Will (0.41.3).** The Possessions tab ends with the record sheet's Will: “I ___ do hereby ___”, kept on the character (script lettering in Italianno, SIL Open Font License).
- **Goldenrod sheet style (0.41.0).** World setting *Sheet style*: Parchment (default) or Goldenrod, after the classic printed record sheets: goldenrod stock (#e7b200), black rules and lettering, heavy capitals for labels and light capitals for headings (Fraunces), and a "1e Fantasy RPG" title in wood-type capitals (Sancreek, with Rye for the digit). Applies to sheets, add-on module tabs, dialogs and chat cards. The character sheet's Particulars box shows the boot from the printed sheet's move box between Weight and Base Move, and the Weapons box title carries the sword from the printed sheet's COMBAT heading, and the Magic Items title the necklace from its MAGIC ITEMS heading. Fonts are under the SIL Open Font License (licences in fonts/).
- **Weapon proficiency list (0.40.1).** Weapon proficiencies are chosen from a list of the system's weapons (and any weapon the character carries) instead of typed. The weapons compendium is labelled "Weapons (PHB/UA)": it holds the PHB weapons and the UA additions.
- **Separate nonweapon rate (0.40.0).** `profSlots` may take a sixth entry, the levels between new nonweapon slots, when it differs from the weapon rate. Used by the Plethora of Paladins module.
- **More options for setting classes (0.39.0).** A spell entry can set `wisdom` (bonus spells and spell failure by wisdom for that list only); `thiefSkills` can be a function of level (e.g. half level), with `thiefSkillKeys` to limit the skills and `noThiefRace` to drop racial adjustments. Used by the Plethora of Paladins module.
- **Attack rules for setting classes (0.38.0).** A class can give `attackRules`, like `missileRules` but for every attack, melee or missile: extra fields in the attack dialog and to-hit and damage bonuses. Used by the Duelist module.
- **Irregular hit dice for setting classes (0.37.0).** A class can give `levelHD(level)`, the dice and fixed hit points gained at each level, for tables like the alchemist's (1, 1+2, 2+2 ...). Used by the Alchemist module.
- **Turning for setting classes (0.36.0).** `noTurning` and `turnUndeadOffset` may be functions of the actor, so a class can turn undead only while something lasts. Used by the Cloistered Cleric module (dismiss undead).
- **Missile rules for setting classes (0.35.0).** A registered class can give `missileRules`: a point blank range and extra fields in the attack dialog, to-hit and damage bonuses by range, weapon and ammunition, notes on the attack card, and arrows per round shown on the weapon. Used by the Archer module.
- **Cavalier rules for setting classes (0.34.0).** A registered class with `cavalier: true` can change the cavalier abilities with `cavalierRules`: the 90% resistance to mind-affecting magic, the save bonus against illusions, the human +1 level when mounted, the good-alignment fear aura and negative hit points, the UA riding progression, and the weapon-of-choice to-hit bonus and attacks per round (by choice, level and whether mounted). The Cavalier Abilities box, the attack and save dialogs and the weapon tags follow them. The UA cavalier and UA paladin are unchanged. Used by the Corrected Cavalier module.
- **Caster level for setting classes (0.33.0).** A registered class can cast a spell list below its own level (`spells: [{ list, progression, casterLevel: level => n }]`); the cast dialog then starts at that caster level. Used by the Welsh bard module (druid spells one level lower, illusionist spells three lower).
- **Setting module API 2 (0.32.0).** Add-ons can now also register spell lists (`registerSpellList`) and proficiency groups (`registerProficiencyGroup`), and hide or show classes (`hideClasses`, `showClasses`; a hidden class stays on characters that already use it). A registered class can give its own attacks per round, constitution hit point adjustment, saving throw bonus, natural armor class, armor class bonus, movement, backstab multiplier by level, extra hit points per die, and split-class hit points (no constitution bonus on its dice, the other class's die halved). Proficiencies have an optional *Base number*: with one set, the roll needs that number or more on 1d20, lowered by 1 per improvement and never below 3, instead of the DSG/WSG ability check. With no module switched on, nothing changes.
- **Rolling ability scores (0.31.0).** A Roll 4d6 button in the Abilities box (characters and NPCs) rolls 4d6, drops the lowest, six times and posts the dice (dropped die struck out), the six scores and their total to chat (DMG p.11, Method I). It does not change the sheet. For a macro: `game.frpg1e.rollAbilityScores()`.
- **NPC roles (0.30.1).** Roles now cover NPCs who serve no one: independent (the new default), patron or employer, rival, enemy or villain, townsfolk or bystander, and ally. Loyalty, liege and wages appear only for roles that serve a liege (henchman, hireling, man-at-arms, associate, ally), as the DMG loyalty rules cover henchmen, hirelings and allied creatures; other NPCs use the 50% morale base.
- **NPCs (0.30.0).** A new actor type, NPC, uses the same rules as characters (classes, levels, abilities, saves, spells, thief skills, proficiencies) on a compact sheet: abilities, saves, class rows, weapons, armor, thieving and class abilities on one page. NPCs start at 0 level (fighter 0 row for attacks and saves, PHB p.105), with a button to roll a man-at-arms' 4-7 hit points (DMG), and tokens start unlinked and neutral so one actor can stand for several guards. A Service box records role, occupation, wages and liege (a character in the world, or name, charisma and alignment by hand) and works out loyalty from the DMG p.36-37 tables: base 50%, the liege's charisma, the modifier groups, the liege's alignment, one special penalty and one bonus, with the loyalty band shown. The Loyalty button rolls a check against it (with the DMG's typical situations and their failure results), and Morale uses the loyalty score.
- **Setting modules (0.29.0).** Optional add-ons (such as the Krynn module) can register races, classes and spell tables through `game.frpg1e.api` during the `frpg1e.init` hook, hide races from the list, and add notes to characters through the `frpg1e.prepareCharacter` hook. A registered race carries its own class limits, multi-class list and ability ranges. A class may be built on a core one (`as: "cleric"`), so it turns undead, rolls low-wisdom spell failure and gets wisdom bonus spells like the class it is built on. With no module switched on, nothing changes.
- **Monster attack buttons (0.28.0).** Buttons are now read from the whole Damage/Attack line, not just split at “/”. Alternatives printed with “or” get their own buttons with an *or* between them (vulchling: 2-5 *or* 1-4, 1-4); lists such as “1-6, 1-8, or 1-10 according to size” become one button each; notes stay off the buttons (“1-4 each” is 1-4, “2-12 + poison” is 2-12); “(x4)” shows as ×4; labels like “(bill)” name the button; printed bonuses (“+7”, “(+6 strength bonus)”) are added to the damage; “by weapon type” gives a Weapon button that rolls to hit and says the damage is by the weapon used; “Nil” and “See below” give no buttons. A monster whose hit dice are a range (black willow 12-19) says it attacks by the lowest until Attacks as HD is set (rolling its hit points sets it). Buttons you edited are kept as they are.
- **Monster art folder (0.27.0).** The default folder is now `frpg1e_monster_art` (was `Monster Art`). A world whose setting still says `Monster Art` is switched to the new name. Existing art is not moved: put the images in the new folder and run Apply Monster Art.
- **Generic names and lore (0.25.0, 0.26.0).** 21 magic items named after characters use generic names (for example Underwater Apparatus, Orbiting Stones, Eye and Hand of the Lich, Blessed Spellbook, Prison of Binding, Ring of Arcane Knowledge, Sword of Betrayal), in the item compendium, the DMG treasure tables and every cross-reference. The lore of the Eye and Hand of the Lich, the Mask of Concealment, the Quill of Forgery and the Marvelous Nightingale no longer names characters; the Sword of Betrayal has its own history as a blade that turns trusted servants against their masters. The Floating Disc and Mirror of Life Trapping text no longer names wizards. Mechanics are unchanged. The lists are in the build tools (`tools/spell_names.py`, `tools/scrub_lore.py`), which the packaging step runs.
- **Generic spell names (0.24.0).** The 29 PHB and UA spells named after wizards use generic names (for example Floating Disc, Clenched Fist, False Trap, Arcane Sword, Compulsive Discourse, Spell Recollection, Magic Disjunction), and every mention in spell, item and monster text changed too. The magic-user Spell Immunity (8th level) shares its name with the UA cleric spell Spell Immunity (4th level).
- **Giant frogs (0.23.1).** The giant, killer and poisonous frog entries had no description (the MM text was never imported); they now have the full MM p.41 text in plain words, and giant frog hit points offer the three sizes the book describes.
- **MMII descriptions (0.23.0).** Every MMII monster description is rewritten in plain words: same rules, every number, range, die and table entry kept. Doubtful OCR readings were checked against the page images; tables the scan garbled were rebuilt from the images (minimals, shades, solar powers, snake poison, termites, webbirds, modron castes and telepathy ranges, and others), and the hordling tables that were missing are now there. Italic “+1” that the OCR read as “+7” is corrected. Book oddities are kept and marked “as printed”.
- **MMII hit dice and hit points (0.23.0).** Every MMII hit dice entry was checked against the page images; all match the book, including odd ones kept as printed (pyrolisk “43”, hangman tree “61 hp/year”). Hit points roll the book's way: hit points written as “hp” or “h/p” are used as given; cloud and mist dragons roll size and age, and the shadow dragon rolls size on d% (25/50/25) with +1 hit point per die, all saving as hit points ÷ 4 hit dice at 5 or more per die (MMII p.56); the faerie dragon's hit points follow its age table; kinds and leaders the book names are offered as a choice (azer, barghest growth, cambions, diakk, fomorians and their young, hordlings, para-elementals, quasi-elementals, time elementals, yuan-ti, merrow, derro, termite king and queen, and more); the froghemoth's tentacles and tongue and the wolf-in-sheep's-clothing's roots and eyestalks are rolled separately. Where the book gives no way to roll (shade, vagabond, phantom, pseudo-undead, swanmay, minimal, hangman tree, pyrolisk), the card says why. Quickling half dice and the yochlol's “6 (1d10)” are not explained in the book: the choices say so. Any entry that is not a single plain hit dice value now uses Attacks as HD for saves and morale. The data settings are in `tools/mm2_hp.py`.
- **MM hit dice and hit points (0.22.0).** Every MM hit dice entry was checked against the MM PDF, table columns included; all match. Hit points now roll the book's way: giants add their point spread (“12+2-7” = 12d8 + 2-7), fractions roll 1d4 (½) or 1d2 (¼), golems attack and save by the hit dice the golem section gives (9, 11, 14, 18), dragons and the dragon turtle roll size and age (MM p.29), and kinds the book names are offered as a choice (elementals by conjuring method, giantesses, flightless birds, herd animals, hydra heads, mimics, rhinos, sea horses, treant sizes, titans, dire wolf or worg). Any other range of hit dice (“3-8”, “12 to 36”) asks which, since the book gives no rule, and the choice sets the attack column and saves. The beholder's ten eyestalks are rolled and listed separately. The data settings are in `tools/mm_hp.py`.
- **FF hit dice and hit points (0.21.0).** Every FF hit dice entry was checked against the page images. The hit point roll (the dice on a monster's Hit Points) now follows the book: fixed hit points (“22 hit points”) and hit point ranges (“1-4 hit points” = 1d4) roll correctly, the death knight uses d10s, ranges of hit dice and kinds (kuo-toa fixed hit points, tiger fly male/female/larva, leaders) are offered as a choice, parts tracked separately (achaierai legs, tentamort tentacles, whipweed stalks, cifal binding force) are rolled and listed on the card, and oriental dragons roll size and age (FF p.28), which also sets their attack column and saves. Entries with no hit points to roll (aleax, githyanki, githzerai, gorbel, hound of ill omen, trilloch) say why. Monsters given only in hit points save and check morale by the hit dice they attack as. The data settings are in `tools/ff_hp.py`.
- **Dragon #35 errata (0.20.0).** The official errata from The Dragon #35 (March 1980) is applied where our source printings lacked it: druids use Wisdom Table II (bonus spells and spell failure); magic-users scribe scrolls and make potions from 7th level and enchant items from 12th; druid *water breathing* is V, S; *explosive runes* saves “None or ½”; the reverse of *cure critical wounds* is *cause critical wounds*; demon type V is AC −7/−5; flesh golem moves 9”; wererat size S-M; giant strength is 19 to 24. The data fixes are in `tools/errata_d35.py`, which is safe to re-run after a data rebuild. The DMG errata and most MM errata were already in our source printings.
- **MM descriptions (0.19.0)** are rewritten in plain words with the same rules: every number, range, die and table entry is kept. - **FF descriptions (0.18.0)** are rewritten in plain words with the same rules: every number, range, die and table entry is kept. Where the OCR could not be settled the text was left as printed; check the book for a doubtful word or number.
- **Naming (0.16.3, 0.17.0).** From 0.16.3 the system, its sheets, compendia and the non-monster descriptions call the game "1e Fantasy RPG" (short form "1e FRPG") and the books "PHB", "DMG", "UA", "DSG" and "WSG". Monster descriptions keep the book wording. From 0.17.0 the system id is `frpg1e` (it was `adnd1e`) and the compendium ids start with `frpg1e-`. A world made with the old `adnd1e` system will not open with this one: create a new world and pick 1e FRPG (Table Edition), and remove the old `Data/systems/adnd1e` folder.
- **Plain-words descriptions (0.16.0).** Every PHB spell, UA spell and cantrip, magic item, psionic power and DSG/WSG proficiency description is rewritten in plain words with the same rules: every number, range, duration, die and table entry is kept, reorganised into lead-ins, lists and tables. Gems were left as they were. Where the source text is incomplete or inconsistent, the description says so rather than filling the gap: Telempathic Projection is left incomplete in the PHB itself, so its entry carries a house ruling, marked as such (sense as empathy for 3 points with no save, or send for 1 point per level/hit die to contact plus 1 to send with a save vs. magic; the sent emotion's effect is the DM's call), and the sheet asks which mode when it is used; the Wand of Size Alteration states no duration; the Detection of Good/Evil chart skips fifth, seventh, ninth, eleventh and thirteenth levels of mastery; the Deck of Illusions suit names are not given, so its four sets are numbered; the Shape Alteration example (19 points) and the multiple-operation range rule (+50% each, "200%" for two) are kept as printed and flagged. The Molecular Manipulation table, which the source printed inside Dimension Walk, has been moved to Molecular Manipulation. Items and spells already in a world keep their old text; drag fresh copies from the rebuilt compendia.
- **Spell description rebuild (0.4.1, 0.4.2).** PHB and UA spell descriptions were rebuilt from the PDFs one column at a time (PHB in 0.4.1, UA in 0.4.2), so tables show as tables and lists as separate lines. A few two-line table headings show as two heading rows.
